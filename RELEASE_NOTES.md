Write with loopmm (the pretrained base model) offline on your Mac: it suggests how your text continues, and Tab or the right arrow key takes the suggestion. Download the arm64 dmg (Apple silicon) or the x64 dmg (Intel).

The app is not notarized. After you drag it to Applications, run this once in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/loopmm.app`
